This is a now page, a snapshot of what I am focused on at the moment, inspired by Derek Sivers.

Last updated: 25 September 2026, from Trento.

Coding agents for generalized planning

I am very excited that our paper Coding Agents for Generalized Task and Motion Planning Problems is finally out! This is the main project I have been leading for the last few months with Tom Silver’s group at Princeton. We gave coding agents like Claude Code and Codex access to robot simulators and asked them to write programs that solve whole classes of planning problems, and found them to be surprisingly effective. You can find videos of what the agents do on the project website, and all of our code (including the full prompts) is here. I am very grateful to everyone who worked on this with me, it was a lot of fun, and I am super excited to continue working in this direction! If you are interested in this, feel free to reach out, I would be happy to discuss it with you.

NeurIPS 2026

QVal was also accepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track as a poster! Congratulations to Sergio, who led the project, and to all the co-authors.

EMNLP 2026

I am very excited to have three papers accepted to EMNLP 2026! DecSelfMask as a main conference paper, ViPlan and SAGE as findings papers. I learned a lot during these projects and I am very grateful to all the co-authors for their collaboration and support. I will not be in Budapest in person, but my co-authors will be there to present the papers.
