This is a [now page](https://nownownow.com/about), a snapshot of what I am
focused on at the moment, inspired by [Derek Sivers](https://sive.rs/now).

_Last updated: 6 July 2026, from Trento._

### What I am working on

My current main project is a collaboration with [Tom Silver](https://prpl-group.com) on
exploring the role of coding agents for physical reasoning and planning. Our codebase
is [here](https://github.com/tomsilver/robocode) if you are interested in following.
Besides that, I am also collaborating on an EU funding proposal, and getting ready for
rebuttal for our EMNLP 2026 submissions.

### Recently out

Last week we released [QVal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32034), a project led by my
friend and ex-classmate Sergio, working in my future lab in Tübingen. Check it out
if you are interested in how we can evaluate the quality of dense supervision signals
for LLM agents! It will also be presented this week at the [RLxF workshop @ ICML 2026](https://sites.google.com/view/rlxf-icml2026) by Sergio.
I was also involved in a project on
[training models for low-resource clinical data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09466),
led by my colleague Pietro.
